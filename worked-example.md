# Worked example: Product Manager, Customer Onboarding

This fictional scenario takes place in November 2026. Maya's source résumé is the record of experience she's confirmed. The posting and its open status are sample inputs; there is no live vacancy or application.

## 1. Start with the source résumé and search criteria

### Source résumé

**Maya Chen** · Portland, OR · maya.chen@example.invalid

**Product Manager, Mallowdesk** · Sep 2022–present

- **E1.** Shipped a setup checklist with Design and Engineering. In a comparison of two consecutive monthly signup cohorts, workspace owners who completed setup within seven days rose from 44% to 57%.
- **E2.** Interviewed eight admins about configuring permissions. Used the findings to prioritize clearer role descriptions in the settings screen.
- **E3.** Wrote the rollout plan for a billing settings update and coordinated launch training with Support.

**Product Analyst, Fenbranch Tools** · Jun 2020–Aug 2022

- **E4.** Built a SQL dashboard that tracked trial activation by plan and acquisition channel. Used it in weekly product reviews to prioritize which trial cohorts to investigate.

### Search criteria

- **Required:** Full-time product manager role, open to remote employees in Oregon, with customer onboarding or activation as a core responsibility.
- **Preferred, in order:** B2B SaaS; ownership of activation metrics.
