# Job Search Assistant

A job-search assistant that uses a résumé and explicit search criteria to find and rank open roles, drafts tailored résumés from verified experience, and prepares employer applications for human review and submission.

This public showcase documents the workflow with one fictional candidate and one sample posting. You can read the whole example on GitHub. There's nothing to install.

[Read the worked example →](worked-example.md)

| Step | What the assistant does | What the candidate sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find and rank roles | Check whether a posting is open, apply the candidate's hard requirements, and rank eligible roles by stated preferences. Keep unknowns visible. | A role recommendation with reasons and a clear record of what still needs checking. |
| Tailor the résumé | Pick the strongest verified examples for the posting and check every new or revised claim against the source résumé. | A role-specific résumé and a short record of what changed and why. |
| Prepare the application | Map known facts to form fields, draft written answers, and flag questions the candidate needs to answer. | A review packet the candidate can use to complete and submit the application. |

The candidate makes the submission. The walkthrough ends with a worksheet, before anything is entered into an employer form.
